The Lighter Footprint is for renters and homeowners who want a lower-waste home without spending more to get it. Our rule is simple: every recommendation has to earn its keep. That means we show the math — payback periods, cost per load, cost per batch — and we name the duds, not just the winners.

We don't do hauls, guilt trips, or "eco-friendly!" products with nothing behind the label. The reader doing five swaps beats the reader feeling bad about zero. If you're new, the flagship guide above is the single best place to begin.
